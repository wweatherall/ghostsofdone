Spindle Calculator

Even spindle spacing for deck rails. Finds the number of spindles that keeps the gap at or under your maximum.

Any box takes 5 1/2, 5.5, 1'5 1/2" or 7-5-8 (feet-inches-sixteenths). Plain numbers are inches.
