Any box takes 5 1/2, 5.5, 1'5 1/2" or 7-5-8 (feet-inches-sixteenths). Plain numbers are inches.

Section

Heights are from the level bottom edge of the siding up to the sloped line, measured at the start and end of the section.

Pieces

Press several pieces together and measure across all of them for a more accurate width. The calculator divides by the count.
